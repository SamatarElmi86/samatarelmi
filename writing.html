Poetry that moves through diaspora, myth, philosophy, ecology, language and the fault lines of the contemporary world.

Bloomsbury · 2024

The Epic of Cader Idris

Elmi’s debut full-length collection brings hip-hop’s music into conversation with British myth, Somali oral history, philosophy and the lived experience of diaspora. The collection includes the poem that won the 2021 Geoffrey Dearmer Prize.

Portrait of Colossus

A searingly forthright debut pamphlet exploring migration, multilingualism, race, family, religion and masculinity. Selected as a Poetry Book Society Pamphlet Choice.
