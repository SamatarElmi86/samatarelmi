Fio Theatre Commission · R&D Scratch · 17 September 2026

After Cordelia

A dark, discordant reimagining of Shakespeare’s King Lear written by Samatar Elmi, directed by Mathilde Lopez and commissioned by Fio Theatre.

Set inside the fractures of a family counselling session and driven by live drums, guitar and keyboard, the piece examines male violence, entitlement, sibling complicity and what survives in the wake of Cordelia’s refusal. Developed through two packed 30-minute scratch performances at Chapter Arts Centre in Cardiff.
