Long bio · approximately 300 words

Long

Samatar Elmi is an award-winning writer, musician, editor and educator whose work brings poetry into conversation with myth, history, diaspora, sound and speculative futures. His debut pamphlet, Portrait of Colossus (flipped eye press, 2021), was selected as a Poetry Book Society Pamphlet Choice. His debut full-length collection, The Epic of Cader Idris (Bloomsbury, 2024), includes the poem that won the 2021 Geoffrey Dearmer Prize. His poetry has appeared in Poetry, Prairie Schooner, The Poetry Review, Poetry Wales, Magma and Iota, and in anthologies including More Fiya, Filigree, After Plath and The Echoing Gallery. He is an Obsidian Fellow and an associate poetry editor at flipped eye press.

Throughout 2026, Elmi is touring nationally with three collections published concurrently: Agen Bien Fou (Kulvert Books, 2026), Reclamations: Disregard Prior Prompt (Aout Fecit, 2026) and 26 Poems for Cash, co-written with Sam Telford (Kulvert Books, 2026). Further books are in development for 2027, alongside After Cordelia, a stage reimagining of King Lear commissioned by Fio Theatre, Cardiff.

As Knomad Spock, Elmi has released Winter of Discontent, A Darker Light and Through the Walls. His music has been reviewed by Clash, Afropunk, Buzz and Record Collector, featured on BBC Radio 6, and performed live in session for Janice Long on BBC Wales. He has appeared on records with Chester P and Jehst. His educational work spans training and development, specialist mentoring, neurodiversity, human capital development and access to future skills.
