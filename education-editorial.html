Signature programme

Neurodiversity & the Creative Process

Elmi regularly teaches Neurodiversity & the Creative Process, a programme he designed to help writers and artists understand how attention, sensory processing, executive function, associative thinking and fluctuating energy can shape creative work. Combining reflective discussion with practical tools, the course moves beyond deficit-based accounts of neurodivergence and helps participants build flexible, sustainable ways of beginning, developing and completing creative projects.

Approach

Creative rigour and access belong in the same room.

Elmi’s educational practice combines clear structures with responsiveness to individual learning styles. The aim is not simply to invite people into existing systems, but to examine how those systems can be made more equitable, imaginative and useful.

His teaching treats creative process as something that can be observed, adapted and supported rather than disciplined into a single “correct” method. Participants are encouraged to recognise their own patterns, make deliberate use of their strengths and develop realistic strategies for friction points without sanding away the distinctiveness of their thinking.

His experience spans international training and development, one-to-one mentoring, neurodiversity and education specialism, human capital development and creative learning. This work is informed by his parallel practice as a writer, musician and editor.

Programmes can be shaped for educational institutions, arts organisations, workplaces and community settings.
