The Practice Compass

A purpose workbook for writers and performers who want to examine what drives the work, what it serves, how closely practice aligns with values and where greater authenticity may be possible.

The workbook is educational and reflective. It is not a talent test, diagnostic instrument or therapeutic assessment.

How to begin

Start with the smallest useful commitment.

You do not need to know the final shape of the work before making contact.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Use the free workbook or course taster to clarify the question you are carrying.

  2. 02

    Choose the route

    Decide whether the need is educational, developmental, editorial, musical, organisational or research-led.

  3. 03

    Make a focused enquiry

    Describe the project, intended outcome, timeframe, funding route and any access requirements.
